Privacy Policy

SiteDims is an iPhone and iPad app for recording site photos, measurements and markups, used by businesses and their teams. This policy explains what the app handles, where it is kept and how to reach us. We handle personal information in line with the Australian Privacy Principles in the Privacy Act 1988 (Cth).

1Who we are

SiteDims is provided by the SiteDims team in New South Wales, Australia ("we", "us"). Contact us at the email at the end of this policy.

2The short version

  • Your projects are kept on your device and in the cloud storage your company chooses (Dropbox, iCloud Drive, Google Drive or OneDrive), under your company's own account.
  • We don't run servers that receive your projects, and we can't see them.
  • No ads, no analytics or tracking tools, and we never sell or rent information.

4Device permissions

The app asks for each permission only when you use the feature that needs it. You can change any of them in Settings › SiteDims.

  • Camera to take site photos and videos.
  • Microphone to record voice notes and the sound on site videos.
  • Speech Recognition to type out your voice notes. SiteDims asks for this to be done on your device, and only uses it when the device can.
  • Photos to import existing photos into a project.
  • Location, only while the app is open, to fill in a project's site address.
  • Calendar, read only and only if you turn it on, to suggest a site address from a nearby appointment. Calendar entries are never uploaded or stored.
  • Bluetooth to receive readings from a laser measurer.

5Where it is stored

  • On your device, in the app's own storage. Sign-in keys are kept in the device's secure Keychain.
  • Your company's cloud storage: projects, the company name and logo, the client list, the plan and the team list sync to the folder your company chose, so the whole team sees the same projects. Who can open that folder is controlled by your company in that service.

Dropbox, Apple, Google and Microsoft may store data on servers outside Australia, including in the United States, under their own privacy policies. The app connects to them directly from your device. Project addresses are looked up with Apple Maps to show each job's suburb and its place on the jobs map.

If your company connects Xero, the app reads only the names of your Xero contacts, directly from the device of the person who connected it, and keeps that sign-in in the device's Keychain. Nothing is written to Xero. Disconnect at any time in Settings › Company or in Xero.

Google user data

If your company keeps its projects in Google Drive, SiteDims asks Google for access to your Drive. It uses that access only to list, open, create and update the project folders and files (project details, photos and their editable marks, PDFs, voice notes and videos) in the folder your company chose. Sign in with Google gives the app your name and email address, used as described above.

  • Google Drive data is read and written directly between your device and Google. It is not sent to us or to any other server.
  • It is not sold, not used for advertising, and not used to train AI or machine-learning models.
  • No person at SiteDims reads it.
  • How it's protected: everything between the app and Google travels encrypted (HTTPS). The app's Google sign-in is kept in the device's Keychain, and project files kept on the device are covered by the device's own encryption and passcode. SiteDims has no server holding Google data.
  • How long it's kept: project files stay in your company's Google Drive until your company deletes them there; SiteDims never keeps a copy anywhere else. The copy on your device is removed when you delete the project, use Settings › Delete Account & Data, or delete the app.
  • You can remove the app's access at any time in the app's Settings or at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Who Google user data is shared with. SiteDims does not share, transfer, sell or disclose Google user data to anyone, with these exceptions, all chosen by you or your company:

  • Your own team: the people your company invites to SiteDims see the projects in the Google Drive folder your company chose, because the files are in that shared folder. Who can open that folder is set by your company in Google Drive.
  • People you send things to: when you share a photo, PDF or project from the app (by message, email, AirDrop or a Drive link), it goes only to the people you choose.

Nobody else receives Google user data: not SiteDims (we have no server that receives it), not advertisers, data brokers or other apps. Because we never receive it, we have none to hand to anyone, including if asked by law.

SiteDims' use and transfer of information received from Google APIs to any other app will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

6Your company controls its projects

The projects belong to the company whose storage they are kept in. The person who manages your company's plan can see the names and emails of the people on it, invite people and remove them. Questions about a company's project records are best directed to that company.

7How long it is kept

Projects stay in your company's storage until your company deletes them. Projects deleted in the app move to a Recently Deleted folder in that storage and are removed after the period set in the app's Settings. Uninstalling the app removes everything the app keeps on the device. Settings › Delete Account & Data does the same without uninstalling: it removes every project, setting and sign-in kept on the device, and takes you off your company's team list. Projects already in your company's storage stay there for the company. A subscription is billed by Apple until you cancel it in your Apple Account settings.

8Your choices

  • Sign out at any time in the app's Settings.
  • Delete all data on the device with Settings › Delete Account & Data.
  • Stop using Sign in with Apple for this app in your iPhone's Settings › Apple Account › Sign in with Apple (do this after deleting your account if you signed in with Apple).
  • Ask us about any personal information we hold about you.
